# Chosen Direction — Operative Ledger

## Design Movement

**Swiss editorial modernism meets a controlled-operations field manual.** The site uses the discipline of grid-led print design but deliberately breaks the page into an asymmetric, evidence-first narrative. It should look like a premium public operating manual rather than a startup landing page.

## Core Principles

1. **Authority is visible.** Every section has a clear label, status cue, or operational role; no decorative ambiguity replaces explanatory structure.
2. **Proof before spectacle.** Tactile evidence motifs, restrained images, and factual diagrams carry the visual narrative.
3. **Contrast creates hierarchy.** Warm paper invites reading; black control fields signal irreversible or high-authority actions; pink identifies routing; chartreuse identifies verification.
4. **Deliberate asymmetry.** The page uses split fields, ledger rails, and offset content rather than generic centered card grids.

## Color Philosophy

Warm **ledger paper** creates an accessible public-reading surface. Nearly black **control ink** is reserved for high-authority steps, trusted states, and the main hero field. Fluorescent **routing pink** expresses state movement and action. A rare **proof chartreuse** marks verified conditions; its scarcity makes it meaningful rather than decorative.

## Layout Paradigm

The homepage is a vertical operating dossier. A thin left ledger rail identifies sections and creates a sense of progress. Major content alternates between expansive editorial spreads, dark control panels, and dense ruleset tables. The feature page becomes a field notebook with large statements on the left and structured proof on the right. The project profile uses a concise dossier format instead of a generic team/about page.

## Signature Elements

1. **Pink routing lines** that connect stages, badge labels, and progression markers.
2. **Receipt strips** containing small status and audit metadata.
3. **Black control cards** with square corners, sharp borders, and a small chartreuse verification marker.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions feel like opening and inspecting a working dossier. Navigation scrolls to explicit sections; tabs reveal operational detail without hiding the primary narrative; diagram nodes illuminate on hover to expose responsibilities. Buttons use verb-led labels such as “Read the control model,” not generic conversion copy.

## Animation

Motion is short, grounded, and evidence-like. On entry, labels fade upward by 10px and diagrams draw their routing lines in sequence. Hovering a control card slightly lifts it and lights its single proof marker. No looping decoration, parallax, or exaggerated scale. All nonessential animation respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography System

**Space Grotesk** carries display headings: bold, compact, and structural. **IBM Plex Mono** carries labels, stage numbers, receipts, metadata, and diagrams. **Source Serif 4** handles longer explanatory body copy to add editorial warmth. Headings stay dense and left-aligned; labels are uppercase, mono, and intentionally small.

## Brand Essence

**A public operating manual for teams that need content systems to be useful, inspectable, and accountable.**  
Personality: **disciplined, legible, exacting**.

## Brand Voice

The voice is confident, practical, and candid about limits. Headlines use plain, operational language; CTAs name the next inspection task; microcopy identifies conditions and responsibilities.

Example headline: “Build a work graph—not a super-agent.”  
Example CTA: “Inspect the approval boundary.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a four-stage routing glyph: three black modules move through a pink path and pass one chartreuse verification square before a final receipt block. The wordmark uses spaced IBM Plex Mono beside the mark, with the system name broken across two aligned lines.

## Signature Brand Color

**Routing Pink — `#ff3d78`**. It is used only for state transitions, key rules, and the most important calls to action.

## Style Decisions

- Display headlines use a dense, structural Space Grotesk treatment: large, left-aligned, compact, and authoritative, with hierarchy created by scale and grid rather than decorative effects.
- Every major section includes at least one operational-artifact signal: a ledger rail label, receipt strip, rule line, routing connector, audit/status cue, or verification marker.
- Routing Pink `#ff3d78` is reserved for transitions, active navigation, key rules, and inspection actions. Proof Chartreuse appears rarely and only to identify verified, public-reference states.
